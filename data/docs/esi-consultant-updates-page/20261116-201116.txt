2027 annual formulary updates
Effective January 1, 2027, Express Scripts is introducing the annual formulary enhancements across National Preferred, Flex , Basic, and High Performance formularies. These updates focus on excluding high‑cost products that do not demonstrate additional clinical value, while preserving access to preferred, clinically appropriate alternatives.
What's changing
National Preferred and Flex Formularies
Twenty-nine additional products will be excluded.
99.89% of National Preferred members 1 will have no pharmacy counter disruption.
View the list of new National Preferred Formulary exclusions and preferred alternatives
National Preferred Flex Formulary
Twenty-eight additional products will be excluded.
99.97% of National Preferred Flex members 1 are expected to have no pharmacy counter disruption.
View the list of new Flex Formulary exclusions and preferred alternatives
Basic and High Performance Formularies
Additional optimization opportunities identified to support client and member savings.

2026 mid‑year formulary updates
Effective July 1, 2026, Express Scripts is introducing mid‑year formulary enhancements across National Preferred, Flex, Basic, and High‑Performance formularies. These updates focus on excluding high‑cost products that do not demonstrate additional clinical value, while preserving access to preferred, clinically appropriate alternatives.
What's changed
National Preferred and Flex Formularies
Thirteen additional products will be excluded.
99.98% of National Preferred members and 100% of Flex members are expected to have no pharmacy counter disruption.

Additional mid‑year optimization opportunities identified to support client and member savings.

As previously communicated, STELARA® SC will be excluded from the National Preferred Formulary effective July 1, 2026, and will transition to non‑preferred status on the Basic Formulary. This aligns with a broader strategy to improve affordability and access through biosimilar options.

No action is required from consultants
