Exclusion List Changes Coming January 1, 2026
This is not an all-inclusive list of exclusions for the Express Scripts National Preferred Flex Formulary.

The excluded medications shown below are not covered on the Express Scripts National Preferred Flex Formulary beginning January 1, 2026 unless
otherwise noted. If there is a clinical reason, identified by your doctor, that requires you to continue taking your current medication, your doctor can
request a coverage review by visiting the Express Scripts online portal at esrx.com/PA.
